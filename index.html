How it works

One loop, every week.

1

You write the plan

Build the week in the browser — sets, reps, load, RPE, tempo. Import an existing spreadsheet if that is how you already work.

2

They train and log

Your lifter opens the iOS app, sees today's session, and logs it set by set. Rest timer runs on the lock screen so the phone stays in the pocket.

3

You review and adjust

Actual loads, RPE, and video come back to you. Change next week before it starts, not after they have already trained it.

Video review

Feedback lands on the rep, not in a chat thread

The lifter records a lift against the logged set. You scrub to the frame that matters, mark it, and draw on it. The note stays attached to that rep.

Alongside video, each lifter has one thread with you — text, images, and voice notes, in the same place as their training.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is MeetPR for?

Coaches who program for powerlifters — squat, bench, deadlift, and their variations. If your athletes train for strength and you write their plans, MeetPR is built around exactly that loop.

What does MeetPR do that my spreadsheet cannot?

The spreadsheet ends at your screen. MeetPR closes the loop: the plan you write shows up on your lifter's phone, and their actual loads, RPE, and videos come back to you without anyone copying numbers across.

Target weights, estimated 1RMs, and trends are calculated for you — you never maintain a formula again.

What does MeetPR do that a general training app cannot?

It speaks powerlifting natively: RPE, percentages, top sets and back-offs, competition-lift tracking. And it is built for the coach–lifter relationship, not for a solo user following a template.

How do my lifters log their training?

On the MeetPR iOS app. They open it, today's session is right there, and they log set by set as they train. The app is currently in beta via TestFlight — joining takes about a minute and is free.

Do I have to change how I program?

No. Program in percentages or RPE, anchor to tested or estimated maxes, write waves or straight sets. If you already have a system, MeetPR is built to carry it, not replace it.

Can I move my existing spreadsheets over?

Yes — the coach web app imports spreadsheets, so a block you have already written becomes a plan your lifter can log against.

What platforms are supported?

Coaches work in the browser, on any computer. Lifters log on iPhone.
