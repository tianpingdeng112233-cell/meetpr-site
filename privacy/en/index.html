MeetPR Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective August 14, 2026 · 中文版

In one sentence: MeetPR is a powerlifting training app. We collect only what is needed to make training work — your sign-in details, the profile and training background you fill in, your training logs, the training videos you choose to upload, your chat with your coach, and anonymous usage data used to improve the product.

All data is stored in our own environment on Alibaba Cloud in mainland China. We use no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, we do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking, and we never sell, rent, or trade it to anyone. You can delete your account in the app at any time, and its data is deleted with it.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This policy is published by Tianping Deng ("we", "us"), an individual developer. It applies to the MeetPR iOS app and the MeetPR web app used by coaches for plan writing and student management.

Please read this policy before using MeetPR. If you do not agree with any part of it, please do not create an account or use the product.

2. What we collect

We collect information only in the specific situations where it is needed. The full list, by situation:

SituationInformation
Sign-up and sign-in Your account role (coach / student), plus one of the following, depending on how you sign in: Sign in with Apple — the user identifier Apple issues for MeetPR and the email address you choose to share (including Apple's private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"); Sign in with Google — your Google account's user identifier, email address, and the name on the account; Email and password — your email address and a password. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes (bcrypt); we cannot recover your original password. We never see or store your Apple ID or Google account password.
Profile Display name, gender, date of birth, height, body weight, preferred weight unit (kg / lb).
Training background questionnaire Training experience; squat style (high-bar / low-bar), deadlift style (conventional / sumo), bench grip width; your 1RM for squat, bench, and deadlift.
Training environment Which days of the week you can train, gym type (home / commercial / specialist), available equipment.
Recovery Daily physical demands, life stress, recovery speed, sleep duration (each a 1–5 self-rating, not a precise measurement).
Injuries and meet prep Injury sites, injury notes (free text), whether you are preparing for a meet, meet date, target weight class, a message to your coach (free text).
Training records Your training plan; the weight, reps, RPE, date, and notes for every set you log; readiness check-ins; post-session self-reviews.
Videos and images Training videos you record or pick from your photo library and upload (including the audio in the video); images you send in chat; training materials you upload while filling in the questionnaire.
Chat with your coach Text messages, images, read status, and timestamps between you and the coach you are linked to.
Usage data (analytics) An anonymous install identifier, session identifier, the names of in-app feature events you trigger and their enumerated parameters (e.g. "opened workout page", "saved plan"), app version and build number, platform, and event time. Event parameters contain only identifiers and fixed enumerated values — never free text.
Experience feedback If you choose to fill in a "what felt awkward" prompt, we receive the text you write (up to 500 characters). This is the only free text in our usage data.
Server logs Routine access logs: request time, request identifier, source IP address, endpoint path, and status code. Used for troubleshooting and security auditing.

What we do not collect

  • No precise location — the app never requests location permission.
  • No contacts, SMS, call history, or calendar access.
  • No Apple Health (HealthKit) integration — we do not read your health data.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no cross-app or cross-site tracking. Our App Store privacy label declares that data is not used to track you.
  • No payment card details — the current version contains no purchases. If we add paid features, payment will be handled by Apple's in-app purchase system and we will never see your card details.

3. Device permissions

Each permission is requested only when you first use the corresponding feature. If you decline, the rest of the app keeps working:

  • Camera — to record training videos for your coach to review your technique.
  • Microphone — to capture the audio while recording training videos (bar noise, cues) for coach review.
  • Photo library — to save a copy of recorded videos to your own library, and to let you pick existing videos to upload.

4. How we use your information

  • Core features: letting your coach write and adjust your training plan, letting you log every set, letting your coach watch your videos and give feedback, and letting the two of you communicate in the app.
  • Training metrics: estimating your maximum strength (e1RM), volume, and trends from your logs, shown to you and your coach.
  • Account security: verifying sign-in, managing sessions across devices, and revoking other devices' sessions when you change your password.
  • Product improvement: using usage data to understand where people get stuck and which features go unused.
  • Troubleshooting and security: locating errors and spotting abnormal access.

We do not use your information for advertising, sell profiles built from it, or make automated decisions about you unrelated to training.

5. Who can see your data

The coach you are linked to

MeetPR is built around a coach working with their students, so your linked coach can see: your profile and training background questionnaire (including injury notes and your message to them), your training plan, every set you log and your self-reviews, the training videos you upload and attach to sets, your readiness check-ins, and your chat with them.

Your linked coach cannot see your email address or password.

Other students

Nothing. There is no way for students to view each other's data.

Ourselves

For troubleshooting, beta support, and data repair, our operators can access the database when necessary. We follow the principle of "look only when needed, look at the minimum amount", and we keep operation logs.

6. Service providers and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. To run the service we use the following infrastructure providers, who process data only on our instructions and cannot use it for their own purposes:

  • Alibaba Cloud — servers, database (RDS PostgreSQL), and object storage (OSS, holding videos and images). All located in mainland China (China East 1 · Hangzhou).
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, and app distribution through the App Store / TestFlight. Your download activity, and any crash or performance diagnostics you have chosen to share at the system level, are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy and do not pass through us.
  • Google — Sign in with Google, if you choose it. Google's handling of your Google account is governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Resend — delivery of transactional email (such as password-reset messages). Resend processes your email address and the message content for delivery only.

We use no third-party analytics, attribution, or advertising SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, or similar). Our analytics are self-built and the data goes directly into our own database.

7. Where your data is stored, and international transfers

  • Storage location: our own Alibaba Cloud environment in mainland China (China East 1 · Hangzhou). If you use MeetPR from outside China, your information is transferred to and stored on servers in China, where data-protection laws may differ from those of your country. By using MeetPR you acknowledge this transfer.
  • Account and training data: kept for as long as your account exists. Training history is valuable to you, so we do not delete it proactively.
  • Usage data and experience feedback: kept for 90 days, then deleted.
  • Server logs: kept only as long as troubleshooting and security auditing require.
  • After account deletion: your account and everything linked to it (profile, questionnaire, training records, videos, chat) are cascade-deleted. Archived backups expire naturally with the backup cycle. To preserve the overall shape of product analytics, historical usage data is unlinked from you (becoming anonymous records that can no longer identify you) rather than kept under your identity.

8. Security

  • Passwords are stored as bcrypt one-way hashes; there are no plaintext passwords in the database. Apple and Google sign-in involve no password on our side at all.
  • API access is token-based, with per-device session management; changing your password revokes all signed-in sessions.
  • Videos and images live in a non-public storage bucket, reachable only through short-lived signed links that expire.
  • The database has daily full backups and point-in-time recovery.
  • Known beta limitation: some connections between the app and our servers do not yet use HTTPS transport encryption. We are completing this migration and will update this policy when it is done.

No system can guarantee absolute security. If a personal-data breach occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Your rights, and how to use them

  • Access and correction: your profile, questionnaire, and training records can be viewed and edited directly in the app.
  • Delete your account: students can self-delete in the app under "Me → Account settings"; deletion is immediate and irreversible. Coach accounts involve plan ownership for linked students, so please request deletion via the contact below and we will confirm the handover with you first.
  • Delete individual items: training videos, chat images, and similar content can be deleted from their own screens.
  • Withdraw permissions: you can turn off camera, microphone, and photo-library access in system settings at any time; the corresponding features stop working and everything else continues.
  • Data copy / other requests: to request an export of your data, or to raise questions or complaints about this policy, contact us below. We reply within 15 business days.

Depending on where you live, local law (such as the GDPR or UK GDPR) may give you additional rights, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

10. Children

MeetPR is intended for adults and youth athletes doing barbell training. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you are under 18, use MeetPR with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian; if you are a guardian and believe we have collected a child's information without your consent, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We update this policy as the product changes. If an update materially changes what we collect, how we use it, or who processes it, we will notify you prominently in the app rather than only editing this page. The current version and effective date are always shown at the top.
